# FlowDesk Promo Design

## Style Prompt

Create a clean, bright SaaS product promo for FlowDesk, an Electron desktop command center for AI creators and operators. The visual language should feel like the actual app: light canvas, soft panels, precise borders, calm blue accent, compact desktop UI, and practical workflow surfaces rather than abstract marketing art. Motion should be polished and efficient, using directional wipes and staged UI assembly to show control, clarity, and reduced tab chaos.

## Colors

- App background: `#F7F8FC`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft surface: `#F3F5F9`
- Border: `#E2E6F0`
- Primary text: `#141B2D`
- Secondary text: `#6B7895`
- Muted text: `#A0AABF`
- Accent blue: `#4F6EF7`
- Accent hover: `#3D5BE0`
- Accent muted: `#EEF1FE`
- Success: `#16C98D`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`
- Danger: `#EF4444`

## Typography

- Product UI and body: `DM Sans`
- Promo display: `Archivo`
- Prompt variables and code-like UI: `JetBrains Mono`
- Use large, readable video typography: 64px or larger for primary statements and 20px or larger for supporting copy.

## Motion

- Primary scene transition: fast horizontal product wipe with accent blocks.
- Entrance rhythm: every scene assembles from multiple UI pieces, using varied GSAP eases and short overlaps.
- No scene content exits before its transition. The transition overlay handles the handoff.
- Final scene may fade gently near the end.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use dark cyber gradients, neon-purple palettes, or abstract-only visuals.
- Do not make the promo feel like a landing page.
- Do not use generic stock imagery.
- Do not over-round UI panels beyond the product's soft desktop style.
- Do not add explanatory how-to text or keyboard shortcut instructions.
